购买与新增设备

iOS / iPadOS 与 macOS 分别购买，购买某一平台版本不包含另一平台。 同平台新增设备的下载资格由相应商店账户和商店规则决定；Clipriva 不提供 独立的设备激活账号或配对码。

同步使用设备系统中的 iCloud 账户，不是支持邮箱，也不一定等同于商店购买账户。 新增设备要取得已有云端内容，需要安装相应版本、登录同一 iCloud 账户并主动开启同步。 旧设备无需一直在线，但内容必须已经上传；仅本机、尚未上传和已删除内容不能凭空恢复。

本地内容与故障排查

即使没有另一端或暂时无法使用 iCloud，本地资料库也应保持可用。不要为了排查问题 先卸载应用、清空资料库或退出系统账户，这可能改变现场或失去尚未上传的内容。 先描述问题，保留原始数据和发生时间。

需要删除内容时，请区分删除本机下载、仅保留在本机和包含 iCloud 的删除操作。 关闭同步不等于删除云端内容。已分享或粘贴到其他应用中的副本不会被自动撤回。

普通客服邮件和附件按隐私政策中的期限处理：无必要继续保留时及时删除， 最迟在问题处理结束后 30 天内清理我们控制的相关副本；依法需保留的个别情形除外。 该期限不是固定响应时限。

Clipriva Support

Updated October 13, 2026.

Privacy Policy (English) · 隐私政策（中文）

Contact: qinhannin@gmail.com

Tell us which platform you use, the app and operating-system versions, the steps you took, and what happened. For sync issues, include the platforms involved, whether Clipriva sync is enabled, and the approximate time.

Do not send Apple Account passwords, verification codes, payment details, your full clipboard history, or an entire library. Redact personal content from screenshots. Share diagnostic material only voluntarily, after reviewing it and only when it is needed. We will not ask you to upload your library to a public link.

The iOS / iPadOS and macOS versions are purchased separately. Installing on additional devices follows the relevant store account's purchase eligibility and store rules; Clipriva has no separate activation account or pairing code. Sync uses the system iCloud account, which is separate from the support email and may differ from the store account.

To retrieve existing cloud content on a new device, install the relevant version, sign in to the same iCloud account, and enable sync. The old device does not have to remain online, but the content must already be uploaded. Local-only, unuploaded, and deleted items cannot be recovered from the cloud.

Your local library should remain usable without the other platform or an available iCloud connection. Do not start troubleshooting by uninstalling, clearing the library, or signing out of the system account; doing so can alter the evidence or lose unuploaded content. Describe the issue and preserve the original data first.

Turning off sync does not delete cloud content. Removing a local download, keeping an item locally, and deleting from the device and iCloud have different scopes. Copies already shared or pasted elsewhere are not recalled.

We delete ordinary support messages and attachments under our control when they are no longer needed, and within 30 days after resolution at the latest, subject to narrow lawful exceptions described in the privacy policy. This retention limit is not a promised response time.
