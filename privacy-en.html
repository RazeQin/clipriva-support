Clipriva Privacy Policy

Effective: October 13, 2026 · Updated: October 18, 2026

1. Scope

Clipriva is operated by an individual developer based in China, identified in Section 10. This policy describes information handling by the Clipriva iOS and macOS apps and their companion extensions, as well as information you voluntarily provide for support or privacy requests, and visits to this privacy policy and support website. The initial launch regions are mainland China and the United States.

Privacy and support contact details appear in Section 10.

“We” refers to the operator; “library” means content you save in Clipriva. You can use the local library without creating a separate Clipriva account. Optional iCloud sync uses the Apple Account signed in on your device. You do not need to give us your Apple Account password.

The iOS and macOS versions are sold separately and work independently. Using local features on one platform does not require purchasing or running the other version. Purchase eligibility and cloud sync are separate: your store account provides access to the relevant platform version, while the account used for iCloud on the device determines the synced library. Clipriva does not create a separate device-activation or pairing account for this purpose.

2. Information and purposes

Clipriva saves, organizes, finds, and reuses content you intentionally provide. Processing on your device does not itself mean the developer receives that information. Cloud sync, website previews, and email can involve network transfers.

3. Clipboard, extensions, and permissions

Clipboard and files

Clipriva reads the clipboard or files needed for an explicit capture, paste, share, or similar action you initiate. App startup alone does not collect clipboard content, and Clipriva does not continuously collect the clipboard through background polling.

Content you submit can include personal or sensitive information such as passwords, financial information, or health information. Submit only information you are entitled to handle and avoid unnecessary sensitive content. Clipriva is not a dedicated password vault and does not guarantee detection or blocking of every kind of sensitive information.

Custom keyboard

You must enable the keyboard in system settings. Full Access lets it access the eligible library projection published by Clipriva to the shared container. Restricted, private, or local-only content does not automatically become eligible for keyboard access.

With Full Access and Clipriva iCloud sync enabled, intentionally using a file that needs to be retrieved from iCloud triggers a download of the original file for copying. Keyboard startup, search, and scrolling do not prefetch remote original files. Full Access does not mean we record everything you type in other apps. The keyboard can use the network for the on-demand download described above.

Without Full Access, functionality is limited and available content depends on the permitted local cache. You can revoke Full Access or remove the keyboard in system settings.

macOS Accessibility

Cross-app copy or paste commands you choose to use may require Accessibility permission to identify the target app or window and perform the requested operation. This is not authorization to continuously collect all content in other apps. You can revoke permission in macOS settings; dependent commands will then be unavailable.

Copying, sharing, and opening

When you copy, insert, share, or open content in another app, the selected content is passed to the system clipboard, current input app, or recipient you choose. The recipient's own practices apply. Deleting a Clipriva item does not recall copies already sent elsewhere.

4. iCloud, website previews, and local-only content

Optional iCloud sync

When you enable iCloud sync, eligible content and associated information are stored through Apple CloudKit in your account's private database for cross-device sync under that account. They are not published as a public library.

When adding a device later, install the relevant platform version, sign in to the same iCloud account on that device, and explicitly enable sync. The original device does not have to remain online, but only content successfully stored and still retained in that account's cloud can be retrieved. Content that was never uploaded or remains local-only on another device does not automatically appear. Purchasing the other platform version does not change your iCloud account or merge libraries from different accounts.

Eligible text, links, code, and JSON can participate in sync. Media/file settings control automatic inclusion of images, videos, PDFs, and other files. With the main sync switch enabled, you can also explicitly include an individual file independently of its category's automatic setting.

Original HTML or RTF clipboard formats may be retained as additional representations of the same text item and synced under that text item's existing rules. Remote images referenced by HTML are not thereby downloaded as offline originals or added to sync.

Turning sync off stops subsequent routine syncing but does not automatically delete data already stored in iCloud or guarantee recall of operations already submitted. To remove cloud content, use a deletion action that expressly includes iCloud.

iOS link-detail previews

Opening an eligible HTTP/HTTPS link's detail view on iOS can automatically request the webpage and related resources through the system preview component to obtain a title, icon, or image. You do not have to press the separate Open button.

Before an automatic preview request, Clipriva removes the original URL's username, password, query parameters, and fragment, but still requests its domain and path. The destination, redirect sites, and resource providers may receive the actual requested address, your IP address, and ordinary network request information. This behavior is independent of the iCloud switch. Opening a locally stored link's details can therefore cause a network request. The entire library is not sent to the website, but a path itself may contain sensitive information. Separately pressing Open passes the original URL to the external app, which may include its parameters.

Preview results may remain in runtime memory or system network caches. A preview freshness interval is not a deadline for deleting all network caches. Clipriva does not control external websites' handling or retention practices.

Displaying HTML and RTF content

Both apps display the existing plain text of HTML/RTF clipboard records and present imported HTML/RTF files as files. Viewing these records does not render their rich content or request referenced images or other remote resources. Original formats are still saved, synced, and copied under the existing rules. If you choose to copy, share, or open content in another app, that app decides how to display it and load resources.

What local-only means

“Local-only” limits Clipriva content sync and associated access eligibility; it is not a device-wide network or backup control. It does not prevent you from sharing or opening content, disable the website previews described above, or change operating-system backup, cross-device clipboard, or other system-service settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Library and sync history

Library content is retained to provide continuing storage and reuse until you delete it, an existing expiry rule applies, or an enabled cleanup rule actually runs. We do not promise automatic deletion of all content after a fixed number of days. Favorites, usage, content type, and sync state may affect cleanup.

Pay attention to each deletion action's scope:

  • Removing a downloaded file or keeping it only in iCloud may retain the cloud original and local item information.
  • Keeping an item only on this device is intended to retain its local copy and submit the corresponding cloud deletion.
  • Deleting from the device and iCloud is intended to remove the library item and submit the related cloud deletion. Cloud and other-device completion depends on connectivity, account state, and sync; offline devices do not update instantly.

Permanent deletion clears titles, summaries, original text, and content representations from the corresponding deleted sync records, including handling those remnants through the app's persistence and recovery processes. To prevent an older device from reintroducing a deleted item and to finish outstanding sync operations, the app may retain technical sync information such as record identifiers, deletion facts, versions, and receipts without the original body. If you choose to keep an item only on this device, the local content you explicitly retain is not part of that cloud deletion.

This is logical deletion within the app's control, not secure erasure of storage media. It does not guarantee simultaneous removal of every copy in attachments, system network caches, operating-system backups, cloud-service backups, or offline devices. Cleanup or sync may remain incomplete while connectivity is unavailable, a transaction is unfinished, or a device has not run again. Completing deletion in the app does not recall copies held by external recipients.

Uninstalling the app, disabling iCloud, or deleting a local copy does not automatically erase copies on other devices, in the cloud, system backups, manual exports, or recipients' systems. Do not send us your full library to request a deletion check.

Local diagnostic logs

Verbose diagnostic logging is off by default. Separately, the app retains bounded critical iCloud-sync audit events and diagnostics for user-initiated copy actions to record necessary operational facts. Copy diagnostics may include pasteboard versions, format categories, image dimensions and byte counts, content digests, waiting times, and save outcomes, but not image or text content.

In release builds, this app log file is bounded by both 500 entries and 512 KiB. New entries replace older entries according to these limits; this is not a time-based expiry rule. Existing entries may remain if no new entries replace them.

Logs use field allowlisting and sanitization and exclude raw clipboard content, titles, file paths, URLs, account identifiers, and raw record or operation identifiers. Disabling verbose logging removes noncritical verbose entries from this app log file; critical auditing can continue. System logs and exported copies have separate retention and are not bounded by that single-file limit.

Logs are not automatically sent to us. Copies you export or send are held at the destination or by the recipient you select.

Support and privacy requests

We retain only information needed to handle your request and delete information promptly when no longer needed. Ordinary support correspondence and attachments you submit will be deleted from our controlled mailbox and local copies within 30 days after resolution at the latest. A valid deletion request can result in earlier deletion.

This deadline covers the relevant messages, attachments, information quoted in correspondence, copies in Trash, and local copies saved for troubleshooting. Moving a message to Trash alone does not complete deletion. For unresolved requests, we keep relevant information only while continued handling is necessary and check whether retention remains needed.

If applicable law requires retention, or evidence must lawfully be kept for a specific dispute, we retain only the minimum necessary information, record the basis and retention period, and delete it promptly when that basis ends or the period expires.

The 30-day deadline applies to our deletion of copies we control. It does not mean data in Google's internal backup and recovery systems is completely erased at the same time. The email provider's backend deletion and backups follow its applicable rules; we cannot directly operate its internal backups. This does not permit us to retain copies under our own control.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, copy, share, and delete library content, disable optional sync, change media-sync settings, and revoke keyboard or Accessibility permissions in system settings. Revoking an optional permission affects the related feature and does not automatically invalidate earlier lawful processing.

To access, correct, obtain a copy of, or delete information we actually hold through support, withdraw relevant consent, restrict processing, or ask about this policy, use the email in Section 10 with your request type and relevant email address. We will handle requests within the applicable legal period, verify only necessary information, and never require your Apple Account password or complete clipboard history.

We do not operate a developer backend for browsing or editing each user's library. Information held only on your devices or in your iCloud must be handled through the relevant app and system functions. Section 6's deletion limitations still apply.

Mainland China users can exercise applicable personal-information rights. Depending on state law and its applicability, US users may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy, opt out of certain processing, appeal, or use an authorized agent. Use the same email for requests and appeals. We will not unreasonably discriminate because you exercise privacy rights. This policy does not waive rights to complain to a regulator or seek other legal remedies.

8. Children

Clipriva is a general-audience clipboard utility, not a service specifically directed at children. The applicable store listing states the app's store age rating.

Minors' personal information is also protected. In mainland China, processing personal information of children under 14 must meet applicable requirements such as guardian consent and special processing rules. Applicable children's privacy requirements must also be met in other regions. Being a general-purpose utility does not remove these obligations.

Guardians should help minors understand the effects of saving and sharing personal information and avoid sending unnecessary identity, health, school, or other sensitive information about children through support. If you believe we received information about a minor through support that we should not process, contact us. After verification, we will stop inappropriate processing and promptly delete the information as required by law.

9. Security

The app uses system app containers, shared-container permissions, CloudKit account-scoping checks, and log sanitization to limit unnecessary access. These are not guarantees of absolute security or a claim that Clipriva provides its own end-to-end encryption covering all local files, cloud data, email, and website requests. HTTP links may also lack HTTPS transport protection.

Protect your device credentials and Apple Account, grant permissions carefully, and review what you share or submit for diagnosis. If an information-security incident requires notice, we will respond and notify as applicable law requires.
